Ang aming misyon

Ikinokonekta ng Sirong ang mga drivers na naghahanap ng lugar na maipapark ang sasakyan sa mga residente na may sobrang espasyo — nagsimula sa Naga, para sa buong Bicol. Bawat naka-park na sasakyan ay isang alalahanin na nalutas. Bawat walang laman na driveway ay pagkakataong kumita.

Ang aming bisyon

Pinapangarap namin ang isang Bicol kung saan ang paghahanap ng parking ay hindi na hassle, at bawat may-ari ng bahay na may walang laman na driveway ay may simple at mapagkakatiwalaang paraan para kumita rito — ligtas, at may tunay na accountability sa pagitan ng magkapitbahay.

Bakit Sirong?

Sa Naga City, Camarines Sur, hindi lang traffic o congestion ang issue — pati na rin ang kakulangan sa peace of mind ng mga car owners patungkol sa safety ng kanilang sasakyan. Ang iba nga, wala pang choice kundi magpark sa gilid ng kalsada. Sirong solves all of them — giving car owners not only convenience, but the protection they need para sa kanilang sasakyan, sa tulong ng masusing verification process sa parehong lot owners at car owners.

Our mission

Sirong connects drivers who need a place to park with residents who have space to spare — starting in Naga, built for all of Bicol. Every parked car is a worry solved. Every empty driveway is money made.

Our vision

We envision a Bicol where finding parking is never a hassle, and every homeowner with an empty driveway has a simple, trusted way to earn from it — safely, and with real accountability between neighbors.

Why Sirong?

In Naga City, Camarines Sur, traffic and congestion aren't the only issues — there's also a lack of peace of mind for car owners when it comes to their vehicle's safety. Some drivers have no choice but to park on the side of the road. Sirong solves all of this — giving car owners not just convenience, but the protection they need for their vehicle, through a thorough verification process for both lot owners and car owners.
